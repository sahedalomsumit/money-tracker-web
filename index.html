Why Money Tracker?

Engineered with modern Android best practices to deliver speed, clarity, and total control over your personal cash flow.

DUAL_ENTRY_LOGGING

Income & Expense Side-by-Side

Log income and expense records side by side in seconds. Comes pre-seeded with 12 complete categories and customizable sub-sources for precise accounting.

SMART_FINANCIALS

Dynamic Allocation Engine

Automatically partition income into custom targets: Saving, Investing, Donate, and Rest with real-time percentage validation and automatic remainder calculations.

INLINE_CALCULATOR

Built-In Math Expressions

Type arithmetic directly into the amount field: 1500 + 250, 45000 / 12, or (200 * 3) + 50 with full PEDMAS order.

SAFETY_NET

Emergency Fund Tracker

Set a target 6-month safety net goal. See exact progress of funds allocated versus emergency expenses spent with custom currency localization.

DATA_CLEANLINESS

Duplicate Detection & Auto-Merge

Logged repeat income or expense rows? The app flags matching entries in amber to fold them into a single clean record, with a toggleable auto-merge setting.

AUTH_&_PRIVACY

Guest Mode & Google Sign-In

Start tracking immediately in anonymous guest mode with zero friction. Upgrade anytime to Google Sign-In via Credential Manager with seamless data migration.

/ SYSTEM_CAPABILITIES

Feature Deep Dive

01 Dynamic Allocation & Emergency Fund

  • ▹ Smart Split: Automatically partitions income into Saving, Investing, Donate, and Rest (`100 − total`).
  • ▹ Safety Net Target: Dedicated Emergency Fund card tracks goal progress, distinguishing allocated savings from spent funds.
  • ▹ Live Validation: Real-time percentage constraints in Settings prevent over-allocation above 100%.

02 12-Month Interactive Bar Chart

  • ▹ Full Year Overview: High-performance canvas chart merging multiple entries per month from Jan to Dec.
  • ▹ Peak Highlighting: Automatically detects your Best Month and lowest earning month.
  • ▹ Tap to Drilldown: Tap any monthly bar to open a detailed breakdown sheet with monthly totals and category shifts.

03 12 Pre-Seeded Smart Categories

Comprehensive taxonomy built right in: Groceries & Food, Bills, Transportation, Healthcare, Education, Shopping, Family, Travel, Dining, Donations, Debt Payments, and Other Expenses.

Add, rename, or drag to reorder categories anytime. Create new main and sub-sources inline right from the entry sheet!

04 Offline Persistence & In-App Updates

Backed by Firebase Cloud Firestore offline cache. Log records without network access, and sync seamlessly once reconnected.

Intelligent 3-tier update check: Google Play In-App Updates API → GitHub Releases → Firestore fallback with customizable release notes.
